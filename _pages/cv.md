## Working Papers

### Unequal Job Opportunities and Well-Being Inequality: A Latent-Jobs Structural Decomposition

**Solo-authored · Job Market Paper · Work in progress**

Develops and estimates a latent-jobs structural labour-supply model to measure money-metric well-being and quantify how inequality is attributable to unequal access and job opportunities, ability or wage technology, and heterogeneous preferences.

### Jobs and Well-Being Measurement

**With François Maniquet · Work in progress**

Reformulates welfare measurement when labour supply is a choice among jobs rather than continuous hours; separates ability sets from job-specific income profiles; and derives impossibility and characterisation results under compensation and responsibility axioms.

### Decomposing Indexation and Tax Reform: Evidence from Luxembourg’s 2025 Tax-Benefit Package

**With Nizamul Islam · Work in progress**

Uses static EUROMOD microsimulation and an exact Shapley decomposition across eight scenarios to identify the contributions of indexation, personal-income-tax bracket reform, and targeted reforms to disposable income, poverty, inequality, and household incidence.
